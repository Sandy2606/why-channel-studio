# Methods: audio (music and sound)

Method notes, not rules: they record how we currently do things and are updated by measuring and testing. Rules live in the owner files (see the master playbook). Copied from the pre-reorganisation playbook (tag `pre-pro-system`), with the original line numbers in brackets; superseded parts are left out and recorded in `history.md`.

## Music [PB:218, 312, 319]
   - Music: pick the least busy track. EQ-carve it (-5 dB at 2.2k, -5 dB high shelf at 6k, lowpass 10k), gain about 0.22–0.24, sidechain duck about 62%.
- Narration measured ≥ 20 dB above the music in speech regions (duck 65%, bed 0.20); a darker ending (track −2 semitones, lowpass 2.6 k) under a cliffhanger (GS film, Nov 6).
- Library: before_the_world_wakes, beneath_the_heavy_swell (sea/voyage, steady build), beneath_the_rising_current, the_last_threshold (dark suspense, builds to a loud plateau after ~75 s), the_quiet_meridian, where_the_river_bends.

## Sound: one sound, one job [PB:120–131, 133, 135–136]
- **One sound, one job ("physical desk-object" world, Vox / Johnny Harris style):**
  - typing → text typing on letter by letter;
  - mouse click → button tap;
  - shutter → photo appears;
  - paper slide + sub_knock → card lands;
  - stamp → ✕/✓ verdicts;
  - marker → circles, arrows, lines drawing;
  - highlighter → word highlight;
  - pin/map unfold → maps;
  - tick/ticker_run → timeline years;
  - page_turn + impact_big → new chapter;
  - heartbeat/riser + music drop + impact → the big reveal.
- **Don't sound every movement.** Key actions and transitions only; vary the family (not one "thud" everywhere).
- **Mix:** voice on top (-14 LUFS); SFX clearly audible but under the voice (duck about 45% while speaking); music lowest; silence before reveals; chapter = voice pause about 1.3 s + music dip + swell + boom, with the card landing on the boom.
- **Script carries SFX cues** next to the mood and pause marks, e.g. `[⌨️ TYPE "1520"]`, `[✍️ CIRCLE "three"]`, `[⏸⏸ + BOOM]`.
- Design animations that the sounds can follow: typewriter text for quotes and dates, a marker circling the key word (amber or ink, never red; → BR-01), a highlighter sweep, polaroid photo drops, page-turn chapters. [PB:132, red marker superseded]
- SFX events come from the scene list and the animation arguments; use library files aligned on their catalog hit points. [PB:219]
- Speech-band SNR check per second against the no-SFX mix (GS film). [PB:318]
