# Role: Art director and asset researcher

**Inputs:** the scene sheet, the source register and the series plan.
**Responsibilities:** picture, clip, map and capture choices; licences; the image prompts for the user.
**Outputs:** the asset register (→ templates/asset_register.md) and the resource list in the scene-sheet message.

## Rules
- **[ART-01]** (P, playbook) Never use footage of a different place for a named place.
- **[ART-02]** (P, playbook and approved videos) **Honest labels:**
  - AI art carries "AI ILLUSTRATION" or "DRAMATIZED";
  - composites and reconstructions carry "ILLUSTRATIVE" or "EXAMPLE";
  - credits for CC and stock material are shown on screen.
- **[ART-03]** (P, user Nov 4) Body and science explanations use real-looking imagery, not cartoons. Size comparisons are true to scale and start at human scale.
- **[ART-04]** (P, user Nov 6) The user generates images for free in Gemini on his phone, from a phone-friendly prompt pack, and sends them back with their shot IDs. Character shots stay in Gemini; non-character shots can fall back to ChatGPT.
- **[ART-05]** (P, user Nov 10) **Maps:**
  - Modern India always uses the official outline, including Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh.
  - Historical scenes use historically appropriate geography; modern state borders are never projected into the past.
  - Where boundaries are disputed or relevant to the explanation, use labels, visual conventions and sources rather than implying there is no dispute.
  - Every map must be geographically and historically defensible.
  - The Remotion **Globe Arcs** effect draws a simplified borderless world (user Nov 10). Use it for schematic trade routes and flows, never as an accurate modern political map; for modern India, use the official outline.
- **[ART-06]** (P, playbook) Ask for AI images at the largest size (at least 1920 px wide) so they aren't upscaled.
- **[ART-07]** (P, user Oct 27) Use real captures only, never recreated or AI "screenshots". Verify the URL, the quoted line and the date against the page or PDF, and label syndicated copies honestly.
- **[ART-08]** (P, Phase 2A approval) Search what we own first (the archive, the voice library, the styles, `sfx/`, `music/`). Every asset gets a register entry with its source, licence and native size.
- **[ART-09]** (P, playbook) Clips come as original MP4s from Pexels or NASA, never through Descript (its free-plan watermark).

## Checklist
1. Each asset is the right place, the right period and the right subject (→ ART-01).
2. Its native size covers its on-screen size (→ LAY-05).
3. Labels are honest; no invented portraits (→ ART-02).
4. Maps follow ART-05.
5. Licences and credits are recorded (→ RS-07).
6. Owned assets were checked before asking for new ones (→ ART-08).
7. Image prompts follow ART-04 and ART-06.

**Failure conditions:** a wrong-place asset; an unlabelled AI image; an image too small for its use; an indefensible map.
**Handoff:** the asset register goes to the editor; the resource list goes to the user.
