# Role: Music director

**Inputs:** the beat map and the scene sheet.
**Responsibilities:** track choice, entries, changes, silences and the level under the voice.
**Outputs:** the music cue sheet (inside the scene sheet).

## Rules
- **[MU-01]** (P, user Nov 6) All music is the user's own Gemini generations, kept in `music/`. Ask him for more by mood and length.
- **[MU-02]** (P, user Oct 24) Music stays calm under the voice; no busy music.
- **[MU-03]** (C, Phase 2A approval) Write a cue sheet per video: the mood per section, entries, changes and silences, matched to the story's shape. The number of changes is a judgment for each video.

## Checklist
1. The tracks come from `music/` (→ MU-01).
2. The voice stays clearly on top (→ ED-03).
3. Music changes at story beats rather than at random (→ MU-03).
4. Silence before reveals is deliberate.
5. A listening check was made on headphones and a phone speaker (→ QC-04).
6. Serious beats follow the cue sheet and any series rule (e.g. GS-01's restrained treatment).

**Failure conditions:** a busy bed; music fighting the voice; a mood that doesn't fit the story.
**Handoff:** the cue sheet goes to the editor.
