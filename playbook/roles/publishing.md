# Role: Publishing

**Inputs:** the final videos, the source register, the asset register (for credits) and `platform_rules.md`.
**Responsibilities:** post copy for each language and platform, covers, and the after-upload settings.
**Outputs:** `<VIDEO>_Post_Copy.md` next to the outputs, with the counts and verification dates.

## Rules
- **[PUB-01]** (P, v2 user Nov 10; v1 user Oct 2026) Write one block per language (English, then Telugu) and per platform. Platform limits are ceilings, not targets: never add text just to fill an allowance. Prioritise readability and relevance.
  - **YouTube:**
    - an accurate, compelling title, keyword-led where natural (#shorts for Shorts; Telugu titles may mix in English keywords);
    - a description that opens with the hook question, gives the key takeaways and the ending, then the fixed CTA (→ PUB-06), SOURCES (outlet, date, headline, full URL; legends noted as beliefs), CREDITS (CC photos with author and licence, stock, AI illustrations labelled) and hashtags;
    - a short list of genuinely relevant tags. YouTube says the title, thumbnail and description matter more, and tags help mainly with commonly misspelled terms (→ PLT-04).
  - **Instagram:** a concise, platform-native caption: a short recap, an engagement question, the CTA, a sources line ("full links on YouTube") and the credits.
- **[PUB-02]** (P, user Nov 10) Before sending, count every caption's characters, hashtags and tag list with a script, check them against `platform_rules.md`, and write the counts and the date the limits were verified in the copy file.
- **[PUB-03]** (P, user Nov 10) **Hashtags.**
  - The English brand hashtag is #WhyEveryQuestionHasAStory; the Telugu brand hashtag is #WhyTelugu.
  - Choose the other hashtags for the actual video and platform, within the verified limits (→ PLT-02); there is no compulsory formula.
  - Never present hashtags as a guaranteed way to get views.
- **[PUB-04]** (P, user Oct 26, Nov 8) After upload, set each YouTube Short's Related Video to the same-language film. On Instagram, point to the full film with "link in bio".
- **[PUB-05]** (P, v2 user Nov 10; follows YouTube's official policy → PLT-09) Decide YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" setting per video, against the official policy as checked in PLT-09, and record the decision and its reason in the post-copy file.
  - **Disclose** realistic content a viewer could mistake for real: a real person shown saying or doing something they didn't; altered footage of a real event or place; a realistic generated scene that didn't actually happen (for example a photoreal AI reconstruction of a historical scene); AI music that is the main focus of the video.
  - **No disclosure needed** for clearly unrealistic or stylised content (animation, our cartoon characters, illustrated or paper-cut styles), minor edits (colour, lighting, filters, audio repair), production assistance (outlines, scripts), or the user's own cloned voice for narration and dubbing.
  - **Ask the user** when a case is unclear. Two examples the policy page doesn't address directly: semi-realistic "DRAMATIZED" illustrations, and Sowmya's consented voice clone speaking a fictional character.
- **[PUB-06]** (P, user Oct 2026) The fixed CTA: "Whenever you get a question that starts with why, just comment it below. We'll do the research and get you the answer." Use the channel's Telugu CTA in the Telugu copy.
- **[PUB-07]** (P, playbook) Never state a claim the video didn't verify. Numbers match what is on screen.

## Checklist
1. Every block is complete (→ PUB-01).
2. Counts and verification dates are written in the file (→ PUB-02).
3. Hashtags follow PUB-03 and PLT-02.
4. Sources and credits match the registers (→ PUB-07).
5. Covers meet BR-08.
6. The after-upload steps are listed for the user (→ PUB-04).

**Failure conditions:** over a limit; a missing source or credit; an unverified limit.
**Handoff:** the copy and covers go with the delivery.
