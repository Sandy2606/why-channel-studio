# Role: Story editor and screenwriter

**Inputs:** the topic, the source register (→ roles/research.md) and the series plan.
**Responsibilities:** the central question, the beat map, the hook, the EN and TE scripts, and the payoff.
**Outputs:** the script, in the production brief, with a beat map: what each scene asks or reveals, and why the next one follows.

## Rules
- **[ST-01]** (P, user Nov 10) **Screenwriting standard.** Every script:
  - creates curiosity immediately;
  - introduces a compelling central question or conflict;
  - makes the audience care;
  - connects every important story beat;
  - builds toward a meaningful discovery;
  - delivers a satisfying payoff that answers what was promised.
- **[ST-02]** (C, user Nov 10) Choose the hook that suits the story; never force the same opening structure or wording into every video. "Surprising fact, then the question, then 'let me explain'" is an optional technique for suitable explainers, not a formula.
- **[ST-03]** (P, user Oct 26) The voice is conversational: a friend telling a friend ("Okay, look at this…", "right?"; TE "ఒక్కసారి చూడండి", "కదా?"), not a teacher quizzing a class. Never open with "Hey" or "Do you know"; avoid quiz-style chains.
- **[ST-04]** (C, user Oct 31) Explain rules through concrete examples of people (what each person gets), not by stating the rule. Avoid ambiguous "up to"; say "or less" or "more than". Cover the perspectives a viewer with no background needs.
- **[ST-05]** (C, v2 user Nov 4 and Nov 10) **Story Shorts.** When a Short is told as a story, a person or family lives the problem, a second character asks the question a normal viewer would ask before each section, and the narrator answers. Side characters are tagged ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE. Whether a Short uses this form is decided per video or series (→ ST-02).
- **[ST-06]** (P, v2 user Oct 30 and Nov 10) **Anna and Chelli**, wherever a series or project plan approves them (not automatically in every video).
  - Cast: ANNA, the explainer, voiced by the user's clones; CHELLI, curious, with a "?" over her head, voiced by Sowmya's clones.
  - Old customs are explained as what our ancestors built around their lifestyle; no line implies only women do housework.
- **[ST-11]** (C, user Oct 30) **The Anna–Chelli film pattern**, used where it fits:
  - a cold open where Chelli fires all her questions, then "Slow down, one at a time", then a title slide linking the questions;
  - chapters, with the characters returning between them;
  - Anna's phone records the answers ("Let me record this"), and he sometimes speaks to camera;
  - an ending with the next questions before the like/comment ask.
- **[ST-07]** (P, user Oct 28, Nov 8) Every script delivery gives the full English and the full Telugu script, in this format:
  - numbered lines with [MOOD · delivery] tags, bold stress words and ⏸ pause marks;
  - a visual beat under each line;
  - SFX cues where needed (e.g. `[⌨️ TYPE "1520"]`);
  - sources with check dates;
  - the credit estimate.
- **[ST-08]** (P, user Oct 28, Oct 30) Generated voice is expressive but light. Use mild tags only ([calm], [warm], [curious], [slightly serious], [conversational]), at most one per line, and leave many lines untagged; never [excited], [amazed] or [fast-paced]. Chelli is subtly expressive ([cheerful], [curious, quick], [lightly insistent]).
- **[ST-09]** (C, approved video Nov 8) Legal and technical scope: the narration stays short, and the exact scope (dates, exceptions) goes on screen.
- **[ST-10]** (C, v2 user Nov 10) One idea per line. Re-engage the audience at natural story turns, not at fixed intervals.

## Checklist
1. The central question is stated early, and the payoff answers it (→ ST-01).
2. The beat map shows each beat asking or revealing something, with no orphan facts.
3. The hook fits this story; it isn't a template (→ ST-02).
4. Disputed history is framed with what is known first (→ RS-02).
5. EN and TE carry the same story points; any difference is deliberate and noted.
6. The duration estimate comes from measured speech, with margin; nothing is padded (→ FMT-01).
7. The format and characters fit the series plan (→ ST-05, ST-06).
8. Voice tags are light (→ ST-08).

**Failure conditions:** the payoff doesn't answer the question; beats don't connect; EN and TE differ in substance without a reason; the hook overpromises.
**Handoff:** the approved script goes to voice generation (→ CR-02); the beat map goes to the director.
