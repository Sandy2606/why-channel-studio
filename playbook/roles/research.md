# Role: Researcher

**Inputs:** the agreed topic, the series plan, and the existing research for the season.
**Responsibilities:** verify every claim before it reaches a script, keep the source register, and flag uncertainty in the wording.
**Outputs:** the source register in the production brief (claim, source, date checked); cautious wording notes for the story editor.

## Rules
- **[RS-01]** (P, user Oct 31) Before any script goes to the user, verify every law, rule, rate, date and number against a source dated within the last few months; search "current", "2026", "amended", "repealed", "replaced by". Never rely on memory for laws or regulations. Put the check date next to each source.
- **[RS-02]** (P, playbook) Record the outlet, date and URL for every claim. Word legends as tradition or belief ("tradition says").
- **[RS-03]** (P, playbook Oct 29) Every on-screen number and formula matches the script's maths and the source.
- **[RS-04]** (P, approved video Nov 6) Label every estimate with its year and who estimated it ("2025 · CAIT ESTIMATE"). In the voice, attribute each number in the same sentence.
- **[RS-05]** (P, approved video Nov 8) When figures come from different borders (e.g. the undivided subcontinent vs the Republic of India), show them in separate panels, each with its own map icon, plus a "DIFFERENT BORDERS · NOT LIKE-FOR-LIKE" note. Cite the exact table and page.
- **[RS-06]** (P, approved videos Nov 5) Prefer the primary or older record over repeated secondary dates. Crop a source capture before any claim we have found to be wrong.
- **[RS-07]** (P, playbook) Use monetisation-safe material only: Pexels, CC BY/BY-SA with attribution, our own AI images, and the user's music.
- **[RS-08]** (C, playbook) When unsure what works, research current best practice (platform docs, data studies) and cite it.

## Checklist
1. Every claim in the script has a register entry with a source and a check date.
2. Laws and rates were re-checked for amendments or repeal (→ RS-01).
3. Numbers match the source exactly; estimates are labelled (→ RS-03, RS-04).
4. Disputed points are worded as disputed, and the payoff states what *is* known.
5. No image or label implies something the sources don't support, such as an invented portrait (→ ART-02).
6. Every licence for a picture or clip is recorded in the asset register (→ RS-07, ART-08).

**Failure conditions (send back):** an unsourced claim; a superseded law; a number that doesn't match its source; certainty where historians disagree.
**Handoff:** the source register goes to the story editor with the script draft; the asset licences go to the art director.
