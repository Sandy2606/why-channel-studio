HOW THE ARTICLE CAPTURES WERE MADE
The browser cannot save a page screenshot as a PNG file (confirmed in
earlier packs). Each article below was therefore saved with the browser's
print/save-as-PDF function, and the PNGs are pages rendered from that PDF
at 150 dpi. They are print-layout renders, not browser screenshot PNGs.
The full PDF for each article is included, except where noted.
Exception: the ICMAI file is the original 6-page digest PDF itself,
downloaded from ICMAI, not a browser print render. Its page-2 PNG is a
150 dpi render of that original PDF.
