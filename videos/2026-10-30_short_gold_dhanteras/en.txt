Indian homes are sitting on about 25,000 tonnes of gold.
Now add up the top 10 central banks in the world. America, Germany, Italy, France, Russia, China, Switzerland, even our own RBI, Japan, the Netherlands... Indian homes still have more.
So why do Indians trust gold more than a bank? It's not just tradition. It's math.
Say you put 10,000 rupees into gold in 2006. Today, it's worth about 1.77 lakh. Nearly 18 times.
The same 10,000 in a bank FD, even at a steady 7 percent a year? About 38,000.
Here's the hidden reason. Gold has one price for the whole world. The rupee doesn't. In 2006, one dollar cost about 45 rupees. Today, about 95.
So when the rupee loses value... your gold doesn't. It quietly protects your savings.
And when you need cash fast? Pledge your gold, and the loan comes in minutes, with very little paperwork.
That's why gold loans are now India's second-biggest kind of retail loan, after home loans. About 16 lakh crore rupees.
So the next time your mom, your sister, or your girlfriend buys gold... they're not wasting money.
They're saving it. For the future.
