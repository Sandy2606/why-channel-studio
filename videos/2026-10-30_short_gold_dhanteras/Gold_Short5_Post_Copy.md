**Title:** Why Indians Trust GOLD More Than Banks (It's Math) #shorts

**Description:**
Indian homes hold about 25,000 tonnes of gold. That's more than the top 10 central banks in the world put together. So why do Indians trust gold more than a bank? It's not just tradition. It's math. 🧮

🏦 Top 10 central banks combined: about 24,400 tonnes. Indian homes: about 25,000.
📈 ₹10,000 of gold in 2006 (₹8,400 per 10 g) is worth about ₹1.77 lakh today (₹1,49,290 per 10 g). Nearly 18×.
🏧 The same ₹10,000 in an FD at a steady 7% a year for 20 years: about ₹38,700.
💱 Gold has one price for the whole world. The rupee doesn't. $1 was about ₹45 in 2006. Today it's about ₹95.
🛡️ So when the rupee loses value, your gold doesn't. It quietly protects your savings.
⚡ Need cash fast? Pledge gold and the loan comes in minutes, with very little paperwork.
📊 Gold loans are now India's second-largest kind of retail credit after home loans: about ₹16 lakh crore.
💛 So the next time your mom, your sister or your girlfriend buys gold, they're not wasting money. They're saving it. For the future.
