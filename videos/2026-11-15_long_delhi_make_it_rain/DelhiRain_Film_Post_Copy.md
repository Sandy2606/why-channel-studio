**Title**
Why Can't Delhi Make It Rain? The Truth About Cloud Seeding

**Description**
Delhi sent a plane into the clouds to make it rain. Not a drop fell on the city. Why? 🌧️

✈️ Oct 28, 2025: flares of silver iodide and salt over Delhi. No rain recorded in the city.
❄️ Silver iodide mimics ice; salt pulls in water. Both need the right cloud.
☁️ Moisture that day: around 15–20% (IIT Kanpur). Winter cloud cover: about 20% (IITM).
🌫️ Smog sits below ~2 km; seedable clouds at 2–5 km (preliminary IIT Delhi analysis).
🌧️ Heavy rain washes out 80–95% of fine particles, but pollution returns within 1–5 days.
🌾 Solapur (CAIPEEX): about 18% more rain in a peer-reviewed randomised trial.
💸 Estimated cost: ₹60 lakh for two flights, ₹25–30 crore for a winter.
📅 2026 trials ongoing; no results published (checked Nov 13, 2026).

The lasting fix isn't in the clouds. It's cutting what goes into the air.
