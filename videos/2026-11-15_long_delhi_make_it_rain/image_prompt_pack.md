**How to make the images (→ ART-04, ART-06):**
- **Where:** Gemini on your phone. Use **one Gemini chat for the whole season**, so it keeps the same faces and style.
- **One image per prompt:** paste one prompt at a time and download at the **largest size** (at least 1920 px wide).
- **Sending them back:** send the images here with their IDs.
- **Order:** make **A0** (the family) and **B0** (the balcony and paper boat) first. I'll check them with you before writing prompts S01–S13. Those later prompts will say "attach A0 and B0".
- **On screen:** every image with Anu, her family or the farmer carries **ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE · NOT A REAL PERSON** (→ ST-05, ART-02).
- **Style choice:** clearly painted, not photographic, so viewers never take it for a real family (→ PUB-05). The science scenes are separate: real cloud footage plus realistic reconstructions (ART-03), not from this pack.
