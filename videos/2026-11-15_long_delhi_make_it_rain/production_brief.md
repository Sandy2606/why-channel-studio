## Central question and payoff (→ ST-01)
- **Question:** Delhi has paid for planes to fly into its sky and make it rain. Why doesn't it rain?
- **Payoff:**
  - Cloud seeding can't make clouds. It only nudges suitable clouds that already exist [C9].
  - On Delhi's worst winter smog days the sky is dry and nearly cloudless [C5][C11][C12].
  - Even real rain gives only a few days' relief [C10]. So seeding can't be Delhi's smog fix.
  - The scientists quoted say the durable answer is cutting emissions at the source [C10].
- **What is disputed, and how the script says so:**
  - IIT Kanpur reported a small particulate drop after the 2025 flights [C6]. Other experts dispute it [C12]. The script says "IIT Kanpur says… other scientists say there's no evidence yet."
  - The 2026 trials are reported by PTI from unnamed sources, with no official results [C13]. The script says "reports say" and gives no outcome.
- **Neutrality:** this became a political row. The script attributes every claim to a named official or institution and takes no side on parties (→ evenhandedness, RS-02).
