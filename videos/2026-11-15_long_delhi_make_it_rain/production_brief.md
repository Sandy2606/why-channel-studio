## Central question and payoff (→ ST-01)
- **Question:** Delhi had the planes, the scientists and the chemicals. Why didn't it rain? And would rain have fixed the smog anyway?
- **Payoff:**
  - Seeding can't make clouds; it can only nudge suitable clouds that already exist [C9].
  - On Delhi's worst winter smog days, the right clouds are rarely there [C11][C12].
  - Even heavy rain gives relief for days, not a season [C10].
  - The lasting fix is cutting what goes into the air [C10].
- **Disputed points, and how the script says them:**

| Claim | Status | Script wording |
|---|---|---|
| Rain in Delhi on Oct 28, 2025: none | Observed (IMD) [C3] | stated |
| 15–20% cloud moisture | Reported by IIT Kanpur [C3] | "IIT Kanpur's director said…" |
| PM drop after the flights | Claimed by IIT Kanpur, disputed [C6][C12] | "IIT Kanpur reported… other scientists dispute that" |
| 80–95% washout; 1–5-day rebound | Preliminary analysis of *natural* rain [C10] | "a preliminary analysis… heavy natural rain… typically" |
| ~18% more rain, Solapur | Peer-reviewed, under suitable conditions [C15] | "about 18 percent… in growing monsoon clouds" |
| Cost of a winter programme | Estimate (IIT Kanpur) [C11] | "he estimated"; screen "2025 · IIT KANPUR ESTIMATE" (→ RS-04) |
