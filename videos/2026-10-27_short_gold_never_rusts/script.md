# Short 2 · Tue Oct 27 · "Why gold never rusts" (Gold week, full video Wed Oct 28) · v2, friend-to-friend tone

Sources:
- Tulane University study, Physical Review Letters (via ScienceAlert, May 22, 2026): gold's surface atoms pack into a tight hexagonal pattern, so oxygen molecules can't split apart to start rusting/tarnishing.
- AIP, "The Invisible Prize": in 1940, after Germany invaded Denmark, the Nobel medals of Max von Laue and James Franck were dissolved in aqua regia at the Niels Bohr Institute, Copenhagen. After the war the gold was recovered, and the medals were recast and presented in 1952.
- Mask of Tutankhamun (c. 1323 BC, so over 3,300 years old).
