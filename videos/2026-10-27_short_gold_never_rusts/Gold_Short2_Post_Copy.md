**Title:** Why Gold NEVER Rusts (and the Nobel Medals Hidden in Acid) #shorts

**Description:**
Why is a 3,300-year-old gold mask still shining, when an iron nail rusts in a few months? ✨

🧲 Rust is basically oxygen grabbing a metal and slowly eating it. Iron gives in easily.
🪙 Gold holds on to its electrons so tightly that oxygen can't get a grip.
🔬 2026: scientists found one more secret. Gold's surface atoms pack into a tight hexagon, so oxygen can't even split apart to attack.
🧪 Only one liquid can dissolve gold: aqua regia ("royal water"), a mix of nitric and hydrochloric acid.
🕵️ 1940: when the Nazis invaded Denmark, two gold Nobel medals in Copenhagen were dissolved in aqua regia and left on a lab shelf for the whole war. Afterwards the gold was recovered and the medals were recast (1952).

Doesn't rust, doesn't fade, keeps coming back. No wonder it costs so much. The full gold story drops tomorrow. 🔔
