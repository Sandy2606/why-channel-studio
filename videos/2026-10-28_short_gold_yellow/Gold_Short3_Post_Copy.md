## ENGLISH · YouTube Short

**Title:** Why Is Gold YELLOW? (Einstein Is the Answer) #shorts

**Description:**
Keys, spoons, your phone… almost every metal is silver or grey. So why is gold the only yellow one? ✨

⚛️ A gold atom is huge: 79 protons pulling hard on its electrons.
🚀 Some of those electrons move at over half the speed of light.
🧠 At that speed, Einstein's relativity kicks in: the electrons get heavier and are pulled in closer.
🔵 That changes which light gold absorbs. Gold soaks up blue light, and white light minus blue looks warm yellow.
🪞 Silver has the same setup, but its jump happens in ultraviolet light we can't see, so it reflects everything and looks silver.
🤯 Without relativity, gold would look just like silver.
💧 Bonus: a 2013 study showed the same effect is why mercury is a liquid. Without it, mercury would melt about 105 °C higher.
