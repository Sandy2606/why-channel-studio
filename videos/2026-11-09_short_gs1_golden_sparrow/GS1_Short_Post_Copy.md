## ENGLISH · YouTube Short

**Title**
Was India Really the "Golden Sparrow"? The 24% Number 🇮🇳 #shorts

**Description**
Was India really the "golden sparrow", or is it just a story we like to tell? 🤔

📊 By Angus Maddison's estimate, India (the undivided subcontinent) produced about 24.4% of the world's output in 1700. The UK produced about 2.9%.
🇨🇳 China produced about 22.3%. Together, India and China made almost half of everything on Earth.
📉 By 1950, the Republic of India's share was about 4.2%. Those borders are different, so this isn't a like-for-like comparison.
⚖️ The catch: India had roughly a quarter of the world's people. Income per person was probably close to the world average and below Western Europe's.
🌾 The harvest was split between the farmer, the landlord and the state, rarely equally. Caste shaped many people's work, and schooling reached only a few.
🧵 So what did India actually make that the world wanted? Cotton. Indian printed cotton became so popular in Britain that a 1721 law banned wearing certain printed calicoes from 25 December 1722, with some exceptions. That's tomorrow's Short (2/6).
