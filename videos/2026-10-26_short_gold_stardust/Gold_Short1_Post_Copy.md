## ENGLISH · YouTube Short

**Title:** The Gold in Your Ring Wasn't Made on Earth 🤯 #shorts

**Description:**
Where did the gold in your ring actually come from? Not Earth. Not even the Sun. 🌌

⭐ Stars build new elements by fusing atoms together, but that process stops at iron.
🪙 A gold atom is about 3.5× heavier than an iron atom, so even our Sun can't make a single grain of gold.
💥 Gold is made when two neutron stars (dead stars so dense that a sugar cube of one would weigh about 1 billion tons) crash into each other.
🔭 In 2017, scientists watched one of these crashes for the first time, about 130 million light-years away. The way the blast glowed showed it had made heavy metals like gold and platinum.
🌍 The twist: when Earth was young and molten, almost all of its gold sank into the core. The gold we mine today arrived later, on meteorites.
✨ So the gold in your ring is older than Earth itself. It's literally stardust.

But if space made so much gold, why is it so expensive here? The full video drops Wednesday. 🔔
