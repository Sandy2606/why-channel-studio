[EXPLAIN · natural] Let me explain. Stars make new elements by **squeezing** atoms together. Hydrogen into helium, helium into carbon... all the way up to **iron**. ⏸ And there, it **stops**. Gold is heavier than iron, so even our Sun can't make a **single grain** of it.
[STORYTELLING] So where did it come from? ⏸

[SERIOUS · twist] But here's the **twist**. When Earth was young, almost all its gold **sank** into the core. ⏸ The gold we mine today? It came **later**... falling from the sky on **meteorites**.
[REVEAL · punchy] So the gold in your ring is **older** than Earth itself. ⏸ It's literally... **stardust**.
