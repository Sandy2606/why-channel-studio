**Rules:**
- **Preferred sources:** Met Open Access and verified public-domain works.
- **CC BY-SA:** credit as required. Because our graphics adapt these images, add a note that the adaptations are shared under the same licence.
- **Excluded:**
  - Freer "Jahangir Preferring a Sufi Shaikh to Kings" (usage conditions apply).
  - NPG Hayman *Clive and Mir Jafar* (needs a commercial licence).
  - British Museum images under CC BY-NC-SA (non-commercial only).
- **Wikimedia rate limit:** the API rate-limits bulk checks (HTTP 429), so each item is checked one at a time with pauses.
