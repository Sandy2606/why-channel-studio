The Golden Sparrow · Season 1 · test batch

Image pack for Gemini

Eight test shots. Make them on your phone in the Gemini app, then send them back here in the Claude chat. Once these look right, the rest of the film follows in the same way.

How to do it

  1. Save the character sheet below to your phone. Press and hold the image, then tap Save.
  2. In the Gemini app, start a new chat for each shot. Use the image model with the best quality you have (Nano Banana Pro / "Thinking", if it is offered).
  3. For shots marked Attach sheet, tap + and attach the character sheet first. Then tap Copy prompt here and paste it into Gemini.
  4. If something is wrong, reply in the same chat with one short fix, for example "keep Chelli's two braids" or "no shoes, bare feet". Keep going until one image is right.
  5. Download it at full size: open the image, then tap Download.
  6. Tick Done here so you know where you are.
  7. Back in the Claude chat, attach the images and type their IDs in the same order, for example "T1, T2, T3".

Leave Gemini's small corner mark as it is. I'll crop it out when I frame the shot.

If Gemini hits its daily limit, you can do shots without the sheet (T1, T2, T5, T6) in ChatGPT with the same prompt. Keep all the shots with Anna and Chelli in Gemini, so the faces match.
