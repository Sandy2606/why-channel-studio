15 - PD03 - Title page / heading page of the 1721 Act, 7 Geo. I c. 7, Calico Act
  File: 15_PD03_Calico_Act_Cap_VII.jpg - 2550 x 3301, JPEG
  Page: https://archive.org/details/dli.bengal.10689.22525 (Pickering, The Statutes at Large, Vol. 14, scan page n344, printed p. 318)
  Text: "Anno septimo GEORGII I. stat. 1. c. 7. [1721] CAP. VII. An act to preserve and encourage the woollen and silk manufactures of this kingdom, and for more effectual employing the poor, by prohibiting the use and wear of all printed, painted, stained or dyed callicoes in apparel, household stuff, furniture, or otherwise, after the twenty fifth day of December one thousand seven hundred and twenty two (except as therein is excepted)."
  Licence: Public domain (1765 Pickering volume, Internet Archive / Digital Library of India scan)
  Note: this is the Act's heading page in the printed Statutes at Large, not a separate standalone title page - the Acts in this volume start with a CAP. heading, there is no separate title page per Act.
