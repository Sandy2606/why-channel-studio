## ENGLISH · YouTube Short

**Title**
Delhi's Wrong Sky: Why Cloud Seeding Fails on Smog Days 🌫️ #shorts

**Description**
On Delhi's most polluted days, the sky is missing the one thing cloud seeding needs. 🤔

🛩️ When planes seeded the clouds near Delhi on Oct 28, 2025, no rain fell in the city (IMD). IIT Kanpur's director said the clouds had very low moisture, around 15–20%, and called the trial "not completely successful".
🍳 A cloud is like a kitchen: to cook up rain it needs enough water, droplets ready to grow, and rising air.
☁️ IITM Pune's Sachin Ghude: winter cloud cover is only about 20%. And during peak pollution events, "90 per cent of the time… there will hardly be any clouds."
🌫️ That flat grey over the balcony is often smog, not cloud.
📉 A preliminary, not peer-reviewed IIT Delhi analysis puts winter humidity at about 1.5 km above sea level at 40–55%, against the ~80% benchmark it uses for clouds that last. It also finds most smog below about 2 km, while potentially seedable winter clouds sit around 2–5 km. Suitable seeding conditions are hard to find.

Next: how long before the smog comes back?
