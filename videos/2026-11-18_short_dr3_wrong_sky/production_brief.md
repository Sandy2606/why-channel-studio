## ENGLISH: audiovisual script
♻ = an approved film voice line reused from `voice_lib` (tag `delhirain_film`). NEW = to be voiced.

**1** · NEW · HOOK
On Delhi's most polluted days… ⏸ the sky is missing the one thing cloud seeding needs.
- SCREEN: frame 1 is bright and readable. Anu's balcony in thick grey (P2 reverse view, native pixels); the building across the road is gone. One label on the grey: "CLOUD?".

**2** · ♻ 0.1 · Okay, picture a winter morning in Delhi. … Mayur Vihar. … A nine-year-old… let's call her Anu… wants to go down and play.
- SCREEN: Anu (A3, ILLUSTRATIVE), a ball under her arm; Mayur Vihar pin on a small Delhi map inset.

**3** · ♻ 0.2 · But from the balcony, the building across the road has vanished. … Just grey.
- SCREEN: the reverse view; the opposite building fades into grey layer by layer.

**4** · NEW · BRIDGE
When planes seeded the clouds near Delhi in October 2025… ⏸ no rain fell in the city.
- SCREEN: the G6 plane (as in 1/6 and 2/6), then a rain gauge stays at 0 mm. Label: IMD · OCT 28, 2025.

**5** · ♻ 3.1 · IIT Kanpur's director, Manindra Agrawal, said the clouds that day had very low moisture… around 15 to 20 percent. … He called the trial "not completely successful."
- SCREEN: SOURCE card (our layout) with an attributed quote; a moisture gauge drains to 15–20% (REPORTED, IIT KANPUR).

**6** · ♻ 3.2 · Think of a cloud like a kitchen. … To cook up rain, it needs enough water, droplets ready to grow, and rising air to keep it building. … That day, the kitchen was nearly empty.
- SCREEN: the cloud-kitchen analogy (ANALOGY). Three ingredients light up on each word: WATER · DROPLETS · RISING AIR. Then the shelves empty out.

**7** · ♻ 3.3 · And that wasn't bad luck. … That's Delhi's winter sky.
- SCREEN: host turn (short), or the balcony sky widening into a winter-month strip.

**8** · ♻ 3.4 (second phrase only) · Sachin Ghude of IITM Pune says cloud cover is only about 20 percent through the winter.
- SCREEN: a sky of 10 tiles; 2 fill with cloud (20%), 8 stay empty. Attributed: IITM PUNE · GHUDE.

**9** · ♻ 3.5 · And here's the cruel part. … On the worst smog days… the days Anu stays indoors… he says, "90 per cent of the time… there will hardly be any clouds."
- SCREEN: Anu indoors behind the glass. Then the tile grid switches to SMOG DAYS: 9 of 10 tiles go almost empty.

**10** · ♻ 3.6 · So that flat grey over her balcony? … On those days, a lot of it isn't cloud at all. … It's smog.
- SCREEN: the "CLOUD?" label from frame 1 returns; the "?" turns into SMOG. A magnifier shows particles, not droplets.

**11** · ♻ 3.7 · The same calm, dry air that lets smog pile up over the city… doesn't build rain clouds.
- SCREEN: still air, with a lid (inversion) holding the smog down; arrows that would rise stall.

**12** · NEW · ★ [C10]
How dry? ⏸ A preliminary analysis by IIT Delhi researchers puts winter humidity, about one and a half kilometres up, at only **40 to 55 percent**. ⏸ Clouds that build and last usually need around **80**. … And the same analysis found one more problem.
- SCREEN: a humidity bar fills to 40–55%, with a dashed line at ~80% (CLOUDS FORM AND LAST). Label: PRELIMINARY · NOT PEER-REVIEWED · IIT DELHI CAS · at ≈1.5 km.

**13** · ♻ 3.8 (second and third phrases, cut after "one more problem.") · The smog lies low, like a blanket over the rooftops, under about 2 kilometres. … The clouds you could seed float higher, between 2 and 5.
- SCREEN: **the centrepiece (the film only mentions this; this Short shows it in full).** A vertical cross-section over Delhi rooftops with an altitude scale (0–5 km):
  - the smog blanket fills 0–2 km;
  - a gap;
  - seedable clouds drift in at 2–5 km.
  - Same PRELIMINARY label.

**14** · ♻ 3.9 · So even when good clouds do turn up… they're often drifting above the problem.
- SCREEN: a seedable cloud drifts across above the blanket; a dashed line marks the gap between them.

**15** · ♻ 3.10 · But say Delhi gets lucky. … Real clouds. Heavy rain. … Does Anu finally get her morning?
- SCREEN: heavy rain arrives over the cross-section; Anu at the glass.

**16** · NEW · TEASE
Next: how long before the smog comes back.
- SCREEN: WHY? question card (kicker WRONG SKY), then the series slide: 1/6 · 2/6 WATCHED · **3/6 YOU'RE HERE** · 4/6 NEXT, ending CTA.

**17** · NEW · LOOP
And it all comes down to this…
- SCREEN: back to frame 1 (the grey balcony), so the hook plays on.
