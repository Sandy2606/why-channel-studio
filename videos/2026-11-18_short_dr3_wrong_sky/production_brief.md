How dry? ⏸ A preliminary analysis by IIT Delhi researchers puts winter humidity, about one and a half kilometres above sea level, at only **40 to 55 percent**. ⏸ For clouds that last, that analysis uses a benchmark of around **80**. … And the same analysis found one more problem.

**13** · ♻ 3.8 (second and third phrases, cut after "one more problem.") · The smog lies low, like a blanket over the rooftops, under about 2 kilometres. … The clouds you could seed float higher, between 2 and 5.
- SCREEN: **the centrepiece (the film only mentions this; this Short shows it in full).** A vertical cross-section over Delhi rooftops with an altitude scale (0–5 km):

  - no wall or barrier between them, and rain is shown falling through the smog in line 15;
  - takeaway label: SUITABLE CONDITIONS ARE HARD TO FIND;
  - same PRELIMINARY label.

**14** · ♻ 3.9 · So even when good clouds do turn up… they're often drifting above the problem.
- SCREEN: a potentially seedable cloud drifts across, high above the thickest smog. No line or barrier between them; the distance is shown with the altitude scale.

**15** · ♻ 3.10 · But say Delhi gets lucky. … Real clouds. Heavy rain. … Does Anu finally get her morning?
- SCREEN: heavy rain arrives over the cross-section; Anu at the glass.

**16** · NEW · TEASE

- SCREEN: WHY? question card (kicker WRONG SKY), then the series slide: 1/6 · 2/6 WATCHED · **3/6 YOU'RE HERE** · 4/6 NEXT, ending CTA.
