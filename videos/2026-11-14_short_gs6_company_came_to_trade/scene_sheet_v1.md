## Direction in one line
**One line from permission to power.** A single time rail runs across the season finale:
- **1608** PERMISSION (cream)
- **1686** FORCE FAILED
- **1717** FARMAN
- **1740s** WARS
- **1757** PLASSEY
- **1765** TAXES (amber)

Every scene adds the next mark to the same line, and the Short ends on that line completed: the company that asked to trade is now collecting the taxes. Visually, this continues GS 5/6's "one line of light".

## Look (same Golden Sparrow design system)
- **Palette and styling:**
  - Ink, cream and amber, with muted earth tones on maps.
  - Museum labels and ink cartouches. No stamps, neon or red.
  - One exhibit at a time, using clean cuts.
- **Full screen with no masked bottom:** captions carry their own outline.
- **Sharp pictures:** every archive picture is shown at ≤ 1.0 scale. Full screen is used only for sources tall enough (the Jahangir darbar at 2,327×3,286); smaller ones are shown as native panels. Maps are drawn at output size.
