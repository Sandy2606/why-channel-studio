Plassey figures on screen are from the National Army Museum (https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/battle-plassey):
- Clive's force was about 3,000, including 2,100 Indian sepoys and about 800 Europeans.
- Siraj-ud-Daulah's army was around 50,000, including 16,000 cavalry.
- Mir Jafar commanded the Nawab's cavalry and refused to take part.
