## ENGLISH · YouTube Short

**Title**
Diamonds Made the Legends. Cloth Filled the Ships. 💎 Golconda's Real Wealth #shorts

**Description**
One Indian kingdom supplied many of the world's most famous diamonds. But its biggest business was something else. 🤔

💎 The Kollur mines lay on the Krishna river, near today's Pulichintala. Until Brazil's diamond finds in the 1720s, India was the world's major source of diamonds; a few also came from Borneo.
⛏️ Visitors in the early 1620s estimated tens of thousands of workers at the mines: men, women and children (a contemporary travellers' estimate).
🔵 The French jeweller Tavernier acquired a remarkable blue diamond in India, possibly from the Golconda region. It eventually became the Hope diamond. Its exact mine is uncertain.
❓ Where the Koh-i-Noor was mined is still debated.
🧵 The surprise: diamonds made the legends, but cloth filled the ships. Textiles were a major export trade, not Golconda's only source of wealth.
⚓ Golconda's sea gate was Machilipatnam on the Krishna delta. Ships came from Persia, Arabia, Burma and Southeast Asia; the Dutch traded there from the early 1600s, the English from 1611; local brokers and interpreters linked them to the weaving villages.
