**Title:** Why Is India Bringing Its Gold Back Home? (1991 vs 2024) #shorts

**Description:**
In 1991, India sent its gold abroad to survive. Now it's flying it back home. Why? 🇮🇳🪙

📉 1991: India was almost broke, with reserves barely enough for a few weeks of imports.
✈️ Nearly 47 tonnes of gold were pledged abroad (Bank of England and Bank of Japan) as security for emergency loans. It worked.
🔄 2024: India started doing the exact opposite. Over 100 tonnes moved back from the UK, the first time since 1991.
🪔 Then 102 more tonnes, right before Diwali.
📊 March 2023: only 38% of RBI's gold was kept in India. March 2026: about 77%.
🧊 Why? In 2022, Western countries froze about $300 billion of Russia's central-bank reserves held abroad.
🔐 The lesson many countries took: keep your gold where you can reach it.

In 1991, India's gold left to save the country. Now it's coming home to protect it.
