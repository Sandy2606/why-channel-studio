## Fact checks (checked Nov 4, 2026)
- Diwali 2025 (20 Oct): 24-h average PM2.5 after Diwali 488 µg/m³ vs 156.6 before; highest post-Diwali level since 2021 (Climate Trends analysis of CPCB data; PTI via ThePrint, 21 Oct 2025). 488/156.6 = 3.1×; 488/60 = 8.1×.
- India NAAQS PM2.5: 24-hour 60 µg/m³, annual 40 µg/m³ (CPCB 2009; Arthapedia).
- Diwali-night wind below 1 m/s (Climate Trends, via Down To Earth). Inversion described qualitatively.
- Vehicles ≈ 51–53% of local PM2.5 emissions, 10–20 Nov 2023/2024/2025 (CSE analysis of IITM DSS; Down To Earth, 22 Dec 2025).
- AQLI 2025 (EPIC, University of Chicago; 2023 data): Delhi residents lose 8.2 years of life expectancy relative to the WHO guideline (4.7 years relative to India's 40 µg/m³ standard). Voice says "about 8 years… compared with meeting the WHO guideline".
- GRAP (CAQM): Stage I AQI 201–300, II 301–400, III 401–450, IV >450. Stage III: non-essential construction banned, BS-III petrol / BS-IV diesel four-wheelers restricted, classes up to Class 5 advised hybrid (Business Today, 11 Nov 2025).
- NCAP: revised target up to 40% PM10 reduction (or meeting the 60 µg/m³ PM10 standard) by 2025–26 vs 2017–18. CREA "Tracing the Hazy Air 2026": 23 cities achieved the 40% cut, out of 100 cities with ≥80% PM10 data coverage; Delhi in the 1–20% reduction group; Delhi 2025 PM10 197 µg/m³ (≈ 3× standard). "This year" = financial year 2025–26.
- Delhi annual PM2.5: CAQM full-year 2025 = 97 µg/m³ (PM10 198), lowest 2018–2025 excluding 2020 (PTI via NewsDrum, 2 Jan 2026). Delhi Economic Survey: 2025 ≈ 100 µg/m³ (data to 9 Dec), 2024 110, 2023 106, 2022 103, 2021 113, 2020 101 (DPCC CAAQMS, Delhi Economic Survey 2025–26, via ThePrint/PTI). "Cleanest in years, outside the lockdown year" fits (2020 = 101 is the only comparable year shown, and CAQM/IANS reported 2025 as the best in 7 years barring COVID). 100/40 = 2.5×.
- Beijing PM2.5: 89.5 µg/m³ (2013) → 27 µg/m³ (2025), ≈ 70% cut (China Daily, 5 Jan 2026). Measures: shut coal-fired facilities, moved households from coal to gas and electricity, coal and vehicle-emission rules, regional coordination (UNEP 20-year review via Balkan Green Energy News); regional joint control credited with 30–40% of the improvement by an expert (China Daily). Note: Delhi's 2025 average (≈100) is above Beijing's 2013 average (89.5); different monitoring networks, so shown side by side, not as an exact comparison.
- "The real fix is year-round and region-wide" is framed as Beijing's lesson, not a policy claim of ours.
- Not claimed: 2026 cracker rules, this year's AQI numbers (conflicting reports).
