# DIWALI · 6/6 "Why does Delhi's air get so bad around Diwali?" · scene sheet v2 (map-camera 2D animation)
Runtime (measured): EN 2:15 · TE 2:40 · loops into its own opening · Telugu on-screen text in TE versions · narration unchanged, no new credits.

## The idea: one continuous 2D world with a camera
Everything happens on ONE drawn world, and the camera flies through it like a drone:
- **MAP level:** India from above (official outline, full Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh inside India), state borders, a paper-map texture, rivers faint. The haze is a soft grey layer drawn over the map.
- **CITY level:** Delhi from above: Yamuna, India Gate, ring road, rooftops, headlights moving.
- **STREET / HOME level:** the camera dives down to one street and into one home: Raju's family flat (drawn, flat 2D characters like 1/6–5/6), window, balcony, the haze outside.
- **SIDE view:** a cut-away "slice" of the sky above Delhi, for the inversion explanation.
- **Evidence ("Johnny Harris sheets"):** taped paper clippings and printed report pages that slide in over the map, with a marker highlight and the rest dimmed (same style as before). Used only for the two biggest number claims.
Every camera move is a smooth zoom/pan (no hard cuts), so the viewer always knows where they are. A small locator chip top-right shows where the camera is: INDIA · NORTH INDIA · DELHI · HOME · BEIJING.

PM2.5 = amber, PM10 = teal, each with a badge. Every historical number carries its year (2025 = last Diwali).
