## New fact checks (Nov 4, 2026)
- PM2.5 size: an average human hair is about 70 µm, about 30 times larger than the largest fine particle. Particles under 2.5 µm pose the greatest risk; some fine particles can get deep into the lungs and some into the bloodstream (US EPA, "Particulate Matter Basics"). PM2.5 is linked to heart attacks and strokes (EPA/WHO health effects).
- Punjab stubble: about 10–15 days between the paddy harvest and wheat sowing. The Punjab Preservation of Sub-Soil Water Act, 2009 pushed transplanting to after 10 June, which squeezed the window. Combines leave about a foot of stubble. A Happy Seeder needs a 60 hp tractor, and hiring one costs about ₹10,000. Straw removal costs ₹1,500–2,500 an acre (CivilsDaily summary of reports). We say "about two weeks".
- Farm-fire share of Delhi PM2.5, November 2025: 7% average and 20% on the peak day, down from 20% and 38% in November 2024 (CREA November 2025 snapshot, via The Week, 6 Dec 2025). Fire counts are not used in the voice, because satellites that pass at midday may undercount fires that have moved to the evening (ISRO-led study, Nov 2025).
