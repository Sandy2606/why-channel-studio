## Fact check (re-checked Nov 16)
- **C21, the strange bit (key fact): a laboratory finding, not a rule for clouds.** Hütner et al., "Surface reconstructions govern ice nucleation on silver iodide", *Science Advances* 11(44), eaea2378, Oct 31, 2025 (TU Wien; reported by Physics World, Dec 10, 2025 = C7).
  - Imaged in **ultrahigh vacuum at very low temperatures**, with water vapour deposited on small AgI crystals.
  - The silver-terminated face keeps a hexagonal arrangement that favours orderly (epitaxial) ice growth.
  - The iodine-terminated face reconstructs into a rectangular pattern, which the authors call incompatible with forming a **continuous** ice layer. The paper doesn't say that nothing forms there.
- **C22, the caveat:** Dreier et al., "Surface Structure of Polar Silver Iodide (0001) in Various Liquids: No Indication for a Surface Reconstruction", *ChemPhysChem* 27(7), e202500904, Apr 14, 2026.
  - Imaged both faces in water and other liquids and found no sign of that surface reconstruction.
  - So what the faces look like in vacuum may not carry over to wet, realistic conditions. How it plays out in a real cloud is still being studied.
- **On screen:** a labelled diagram ("illustration · based on a 2025 lab study"), not a microscope image. Both studies are cited.
- **C9 (WMO):**
  - Seeding can't create rain clouds; it works on suitable clouds that are already there.
  - The evidence for hygroscopic (salt) seeding is weaker than for glaciogenic seeding.
  - Line 13 says only what the WMO says.
- **C19:** the freezer bottle "can" stay liquid; it doesn't always happen. The line keeps "can".
- **C20:** salt clumping (hygroscopic) is an everyday analogy, and is labelled as an analogy.

## ENGLISH: audiovisual script
♻ = an approved film voice line reused from `voice_lib` (tag `delhirain_film`). NEW = to be voiced.

**1** · NARRATOR over picture, then HOST on "How?" · NEW
Scientists fire something that looks like **smoke** into a cloud… ⏸ and under the **right conditions**, it can help that cloud **rain**. ⏸ How?
- SCREEN:
  - Frame 1 is G6, the plane wing with burning flares, white smoke streaming into grey cloud. It is bright and instantly readable.
  - The camera follows the smoke into the cloud (push on native pixels).
  - On "How?" cut to the host at the board, one card pinned: "SMOKE → RAIN?". He tilts his head and asks.
- WHY: the paradox in one line, without claiming the smoke turns into rain. The particles may help rain form, if the cloud is right.
- SCREEN NOTE: the smoke trail is drawn as tiny particles entering the cloud, never as smoke becoming drops.

**2** · ♻ 2.1 · Here's the thing. ⏸ A cloud is just **billions** of tiny water droplets… so light they **float**, like dust in a sunbeam.
- SCREEN: the camera flies into the S1b monsoon cloud. The paint dissolves into a macro world of floating droplets, with a sunbeam crossing them and dust-like specks drifting. Key term: BILLIONS OF DROPLETS.

**3** · ♻ 2.2 · To fall as rain, they have to **grow**. ⏸ Heavier and heavier… until they drop.
- SCREEN: one droplet collects its neighbours and swells. A small weight icon tips, and it falls out of frame.

**4** · ♻ 2.3 · And high up, a lot of clouds are colder than **zero**. ⏸ But the droplets… don't freeze. [C9]
- SCREEN: the camera rises. A thermometer slides up the left edge and drops below 0 °C, and the droplets turn cold blue but stay round and liquid. Label: BELOW 0 °C · STILL LIQUID.

**5** · ♻ 2.4 · [curious] You may have seen this at home. ⏸ A bottle of water forgotten in the freezer can stay **liquid**, below zero… and then you tap it, and it turns to **ice** in seconds. [C19]
- SCREEN:
  - Human moment: R4a, the bottle in a home freezer (film art), large.
  - On "tap", a finger-tap ripple, then ice races down the bottle as a wipe from R4a to R4b, the frozen slush.
  - Labels: ANALOGY · and "it can happen, not always". The freeze takes the real pace of the analogy (a few seconds), not an instant flash.

**6** · ♻ 2.5 · That's called **supercooled** water. ⏸ It's waiting for something to freeze **on**. [C19][C9]
- SCREEN: back in the cloud. Key term SUPERCOOLED lands big. The droplets tremble, each with a faint empty hexagon outline: "waiting".

**7** · ♻ 2.6 · That's where **silver iodide** comes in. ⏸ Its crystal looks almost exactly like **ice**: the same six-sided pattern, with its atoms spaced almost the same way. [C7]
- SCREEN:
  - A particle drifts in from the smoke trail, and a magnifier opens on it.
  - Two lattices side by side: ICE (pale blue hexagons) and SILVER IODIDE (silver and violet atoms). The AgI lattice slides under the ice lattice and the spacing lines align.
  - SOURCE card: lattice match, standard cloud physics; the AgI–ice match described in Hütner et al., *Science Advances*, 2025.

**8** · HOST, then diagram · NEW · [leaning in] Here's the **strange** bit: ⏸ in a 2025 **lab** study, the crystal's two faces didn't behave the same. ⏸ On the **silver** side, ice grew in neat layers. ⏸ On the **iodine** side, it didn't. ⏸ What happens in a **real cloud** is still being studied. [C21][C22]
- SCREEN:
  1. "Here's the strange bit": host close, eyebrows up (≈1.5 s).
  2. A lab chamber tag lands: LAB TEST · ULTRAHIGH VACUUM · VERY COLD.
  3. The crystal splits into two faces, side by side.
  4. SILVER FACE: a hexagonal pattern; water settles into an orderly ice layer that spreads across it, flat and regular.
  5. IODINE FACE: rectangular pattern; water still lands, but only in scattered, uneven patches that never join into a continuous layer. Label: NO CONTINUOUS ICE LAYER. Something forms; it just isn't orderly.
  6. On "real cloud", a second card: 2026 STUDY IN WATER: NO SUCH RESHAPING SEEN, with the source line *ChemPhysChem*, Apr 2026. Then a question mark over a real cloud.
  7. Label throughout: ILLUSTRATION · BASED ON LAB STUDIES (Science Advances 2025; ChemPhysChem 2026).
- WHY: keeps the surprising discovery (the re-hook) while saying plainly that it's a lab result and the cloud picture is still open.

**9** · ♻ 2.7 · So the water treats it like ice. ⏸ It's a **template**. ⏸ Crystals form on it, grow, get heavy, fall… and melt into **rain** on the way down.
- SCREEN: shown on the particle itself in one move: ice crystal → grows by drawing on nearby droplets → falls through a dashed 0 °C line → becomes a raindrop. Each stage gets a short label on its word. Neighbouring droplets visibly shrink as the crystal grows, so growth has a source; nothing appears from nowhere.

**10** · ♻ 2.8 · Warmer clouds get **salt** instead. ⏸ And you know how salt behaves: leave the jar open in the monsoon and it goes **damp** and **lumpy**. ⏸ It pulls water right out of the air. [C20][C9]
- SCREEN:
  - Split: COLD CLOUD (silver iodide) on top, WARMER CLOUD (salt) below. Camera to the salt half.
  - Human moment: the kitchen salt jar, dry (R5a) → humid monsoon air → clumped (R5b), on the words.
  - Water-vapour arrows flow into the salt. Labels: ANALOGY · PULLS WATER FROM THE AIR. The clumping takes its real slow pace (time-lapse tag "over hours"), not a snap.

**11** · ♻ 2.9 · In a cloud, that pull helps droplets grow **bigger, faster**. [C9][C15]
- SCREEN: a salt grain becomes a slightly larger droplet. It drifts, collides with smaller droplets and merges with some (collision and coalescence); some near-misses bounce past. It **may** grow big enough to fall. No race, no winner, no numbers. Label: CAN HELP DROPLETS GROW.

**12** · HOST · ♻ 2.10 · [slightly serious] But look at what both tricks need. ⏸ A **cloud**. ⏸ And the **right** kind of cloud. [C9]
- SCREEN:
  - The host at the board (human turn).
  - On "A cloud", a full-frame checklist forms from both tricks: ✓ A CLOUD · ✓ THE RIGHT KIND.

**13** · ♻ 2.11 · The World Meteorological Organization is clear about this: seeding can't **create** clouds that rain. ⏸ It only works on clouds that are **already there**. [C9]
- SCREEN:
  - SOURCE card: WMO Statement on Weather Modification (updated Jul 2026).
  - Beneath it, a split:
    - left: a plane seeds a clear blue sky → nothing to work on (label: NO CLOUD, NO RAIN);
    - right: a plane seeds a suitable existing cloud → the cloud **may** respond: some extra crystals and droplets grow, a little more rain **may** fall (label: MAY RESPOND · NOT GUARANTEED).

**14** · NARRATOR · NEW · series slide line
So why didn't it work over **Delhi**? ⏸ Next: Delhi's **wrong sky**.
- SCREEN:
  - WHY? reveal on "why", with kicker 2025.
  - Then the series slide: 1/6 done, YOU'RE HERE 2/6, NEXT 3/6 "The wrong sky", the full-film pointer, and the Subscribe/Follow card.
