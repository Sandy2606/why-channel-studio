## ENGLISH · YouTube Short

**Title**
Can Smoke Make It Rain? The Crystal Inside the Flare 🌧️ #shorts

**Description**
Planes fire something that looks like smoke into clouds. Under the right conditions, it can help a cloud rain. How? 🤔

💧 Clouds are billions of tiny droplets, too light to fall. High up, many are colder than 0 °C and still liquid: supercooled, waiting for something to freeze on.
❄️ Silver iodide's crystal looks almost like ice: the same six-sided pattern, atoms spaced almost the same way. Ice can form on it, grow, fall and melt into rain.
🔬 The strange bit: in a 2025 lab study (ultrahigh vacuum, very cold), the crystal's two faces behaved differently. Ice grew in neat layers on the silver side, not on the iodine side. A 2026 study in water saw no such reshaping, so what happens in a real cloud is still being studied.
🧂 Warmer clouds get salt instead: it pulls water from the air, which can help droplets grow bigger.
☁️ Both tricks need a cloud, and the right kind. The WMO is clear: seeding can't create rain clouds; it works only on suitable clouds already there, and they may respond, not always.

So why didn't it work over Delhi? Next: Delhi's wrong sky.
