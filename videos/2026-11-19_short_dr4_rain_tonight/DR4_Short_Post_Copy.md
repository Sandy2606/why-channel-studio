## ENGLISH · YouTube Short

**Title**
Rain Tonight, Smog By…? How Long Delhi's Clean Air Lasts 🌧️ #shorts

**Description**
Suppose Delhi gets heavy rain tonight. How long before the smog comes back? 🤔

🌧️ Heavy natural rain really helps: a preliminary IIT Delhi analysis says it can wash out 80–95% of fine particles. A light drizzle barely changes anything.
📅 But even after a big storm, pollution typically came back within 1–5 days, and not all at once: in that analysis, gases like NOx and SO₂ typically returned in about 2–3 days, particles (PM2.5, PM10) in about 4–5. These are typical findings, not deadlines; they vary with the weather.
🚗 Rain doesn't switch off what makes the smog. The next morning, traffic, dust and smoke start filling the air again.
📆 IITM Pune's Sachin Ghude: to get Delhi's air from "very poor" to "moderate", you'd need rain almost every other day.
☁️ And artificial rain? CAIPEEX lead Thara Prabhakaran: "no documented scientific evidence yet that cloud seeding can reduce pollution." IIT Kanpur reported a small drop in particles after the 2025 flights; other scientists dispute it, and it hasn't been shown that seeding caused it.

So is cloud seeding just fake? Not at all. In Solapur, it added rain to suitable clouds. That's rainfall enhancement, not proof it cleans Delhi's air. Next: Solapur.
