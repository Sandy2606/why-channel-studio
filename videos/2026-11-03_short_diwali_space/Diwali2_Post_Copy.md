## ENGLISH · YouTube Short

**Title:** That "NASA Diwali Photo" of India? It's NOT Diwali. #shorts

**Description:**
Every Diwali, this photo of India glowing in every colour comes back on WhatsApp. "NASA's Diwali photo!" It's not. 🛰️

🎨 It's a real satellite image, but it's a 2003 composite made by NOAA scientist Chris Elvidge from years of US Defense Meteorological Satellite Program data.
📅 The colours are years, not fireworks: white = lights already there before 1992, blue = new in 1992, green = 1998, red = 2003. It's a map of India's cities growing, not one night.
🌙 Those satellites were built to see clouds at night by moonlight, and city lights showed up too.
🟠 NASA's real image from Diwali week (Nov 12, 2012) is orange and white, like street lights. No rainbow.
🔭 NASA: "any extra light produced during Diwali is so subtle that it is likely imperceptible when observed from space." The satellite flies about 824 km up, and one pixel covers roughly 750 m, a whole neighbourhood.

So next time Raju uncle forwards it, reply: "Beautiful map. Wrong night." 😄
