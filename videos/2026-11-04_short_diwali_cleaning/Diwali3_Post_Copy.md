## ENGLISH · YouTube Short

**Title:** Why Every Indian Home Gets Deep-Cleaned Before Diwali #shorts

**Description:**
Cupboards out. Fans down. Walls painted. Why right before Diwali? 🧹

🪔 Many families believe Lakshmi visits a clean, bright home on Diwali night. The timing also has a practical side.
📅 This year the southwest monsoon withdrew from the entire country on Oct 15 (IMD). Diwali is Nov 8, 24 days later.
💧 Across much of India, months of monsoon damp have just ended: damp walls, black spots, that cupboard smell.
🦠 Mould needs moisture. The US EPA says to keep indoor humidity below 60% (ideally 30–50%) to help prevent it.
⚪ Our grandparents painted with lime (sunnam). Fresh slaked lime is strongly alkaline, about pH 12.4, and that alkalinity can inactivate many bacteria and viruses.
🪨 Over time it soaks up CO₂ from the air and hardens into calcium carbonate, the main mineral in limestone.

Note: South India is different. The northeast monsoon (Oct–Dec) is the main rainy season for Tamil Nadu and also brings rain to Coastal Andhra Pradesh, Rayalaseema, Kerala and South Interior Karnataka.

The belief is cultural. The timing also makes practical sense. So the habit stayed.
