## ENGLISH
1. [conversational] Every year, right before Diwali, the whole house gets turned upside down. … Cupboards out. Fans down. Walls painted. … Why right now?
   → frame 0: cartoon home mid-chaos (cupboard open, fan blades, paint roller); title "WHY RIGHT NOW?"
2. Many families believe Lakshmi visits a clean, bright home on Diwali night. … But the timing has a practical side too.
   → diya-lit clean doorway with rangoli; card "BELIEF" → flips to "TIMING"
3. Look at the calendar. … This year, the monsoon left India on October 15th. Diwali is November 8th.
   → calendar strip: OCT 15 "MONSOON GONE" (IMD clipping flash) … NOV 8 "DIWALI", gap highlighted "3 WEEKS"
4. That's four months of rain, just ending. … Damp walls. Black spots in the corners. That smell in the cupboards.
   → wall with spreading black mould spots; cupboard with stink lines
5. Mould needs moisture. … The US EPA says to keep indoor humidity under 60 percent to stop it. Four months of rain makes that hard.
   → humidity meter needle in the red; EPA evidence flash "below 60 percent"
6. So the first dry weeks after the rain… are the best time to clean, dry, and paint.
   → window opens, sun, meter drops into green; DRY ✓
7. And our grandparents' paint? … Lime. Sunnam.
   → old lime-washed house, bucket of white lime, brush
8. Wet lime has a pH of about 12.4. … That's strong enough to kill many germs.
   → pH scale 0–14 slides to 12.4; germ icons pop; evidence flash (Scientific Reports, 2021)
9. Then it soaks up carbon dioxide from the air… and slowly turns into calcium carbonate. The same stuff as limestone.
   → CO₂ bubbles drift into the wall; label Ca(OH)₂ → CaCO₃ = LIMESTONE
10. So every year, the walls got a fresh coat of germ-killer… that turned back into stone.
   → wall cross-section hardening; stamp "GERM-KILLER → STONE"
11. Today we use paint and tiles… ⏸ but the timing still works. So the habit stayed.
   → split: lime-washed home / modern flat, both being cleaned
12. Tomorrow: India's stock market opens on a Sunday, on Diwali… for one hour.

L. So this weekend, when Amma hands you a broom…

## Fact checks (checked Nov 3, 2026)
- Monsoon withdrew from the entire country on 15 Oct 2026 (IMD press release, 15 Oct 2026). Normal date 15 Oct (Tribune, Oct 16 2025). Diwali (Lakshmi Puja) Sun 8 Nov 2026.
- EPA: "Indoor relative humidity (RH) should be kept below 60 percent -- ideally between 30 percent and 50 percent"; mould grows where moisture and oxygen are present.
- Ca(OH)₂ pH 12.4 at 25 °C; inactivates E. coli, Salmonella, avian influenza; reacts with CO₂ in air to form calcium carbonate, losing its disinfecting activity (Matsuzaki et al., Scientific Reports 11:7941, 2021).
- Lime (quicklime) is made by burning limestone; calcium carbonate is the main mineral in limestone and chalk (standard chemistry).
- Lakshmi belief stated as a belief ("many families believe").
- Teaser fixed: Muhurat trading is Sun 8 Nov 2026, 2:20–3:30 PM, so "on a Sunday, on Diwali", not "at night".
