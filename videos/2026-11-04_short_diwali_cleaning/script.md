   → wall with spreading black mould spots; cupboard with stink lines
5. Mould needs moisture. … The US EPA says to keep indoor humidity under 60 percent to help prevent it. Months of rain make that hard.
   → humidity meter needle in the red; EPA evidence flash "below 60 percent"
6. So in many places, the weeks after the monsoon… are a natural time to clean, dry, and paint.
   → window opens, sun, meter drops into green

7. And our grandparents' paint? … Lime. Sunnam.

8. Fresh lime is strongly alkaline, about pH 12.4. … That can kill many germs.
   → pH scale 0–14 slides to 12.4; germ icons pop; evidence flash (Scientific Reports, 2021)
9. So a fresh coat of lime wasn't just white. … For a while, it also fought germs.
   → wall glows white; germ icons fade
10. Then it soaks up carbon dioxide from the air… and slowly hardens into calcium carbonate, the main mineral in limestone.
   → CO₂ bubbles drift into the wall; Ca(OH)₂ → CaCO₃; label "LIME → LIMESTONE MINERAL"
11. The belief is cultural. … But the timing also makes practical sense. So the habit stayed.

## Fact checks (checked Nov 3, 2026)
- Southwest monsoon withdrew from the entire country on 15 Oct 2026 (IMD press release). Normal date 15 Oct. Oct 15 → Nov 8 = 24 days. South India (Tamil Nadu, coastal Andhra, Rayalaseema, Kerala, south interior Karnataka) gets the northeast monsoon Oct–Dec, hence "much of India" / "many places" + on-screen note.
- EPA: keep indoor RH below 60 percent, ideally 30–50 percent; mould grows where moisture and oxygen are present ("help prevent", not "stop").
- Ca(OH)₂ pH 12.4 at 25 °C; inactivates E. coli, Salmonella, avian influenza; reacts with CO₂ to calcium carbonate, losing its disinfecting activity (Matsuzaki et al., Scientific Reports 11:7941, 2021). The Short does NOT claim limewash was the historical reason for the custom.
- Calcium carbonate is the main mineral in limestone (standard chemistry).
- Lakshmi belief stated as a belief.
- Muhurat trading Sun 8 Nov 2026: normal market 2:20–3:30 PM = 70 minutes (pre-open from 2:05 PM).
