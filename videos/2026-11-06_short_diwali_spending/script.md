## Fact checks (checked Nov 3, 2026)
- 2026 forecast: CAIT expects festive trade (Navratri → Diwali) to "exceed ₹7 lakh crore", +15–18% on last year; services expected ₹80,000 cr–₹1 lakh cr (IANS via Investment Guru India; Business Today, both 14 Oct 2026). This is a forecast by a traders' body.
- Category shares: CAIT's preliminary 2026 estimates use the same shares as its 2025 report: groceries & FMCG 12%, gold/silver/jewellery 10%, electronics & electricals 8%, consumer durables / ready-made garments / gift items 7% each, home décor and furniture & furnishings 5% each (IANS, 14 Oct 2026). Sweets & namkeen were 5% in the 2025 report (CAIT). These are shares of GOODS sales (they sum to 100% with "miscellaneous" 19%), so "100 rupees of the goods people buy" is the right framing.
- 2025 actuals (CAIT report, survey of 60 distribution centres): ₹6.05 lakh crore = ₹5.40 lakh crore goods + ₹65,000 crore services; 85% of trade through mainline/non-corporate/traditional markets; ~50 lakh temporary jobs.
- Per-person line dropped in v2 (a trade forecast is not average household spending).
- Groceries & FMCG examples exclude dry fruits (CAIT counts fruits & dry fruits separately, 3%). The nine named categories total ₹66 (12+10+8+7×3+5×3), so EVERYTHING ELSE = ₹34. Sweets & namkeen 5% is from the 2025 report; the 2026 preliminary list groups sweets with the remaining categories.
- The ₹7 lakh crore headline includes services (2025: ₹6.05 lakh crore = ₹5.40 goods + ₹0.65 services). 2026 services forecast ₹80,000 cr–₹1 lakh cr, so "roughly 80,000 crore" is the low end.
- 85% = CAIT's "mainline retail, especially non-corporate and traditional markets", not "small local shops" exactly; Lakshmi aunty is an example, not the 85%.
- "Not gold": groceries & FMCG 12% > gold/silver/jewellery 10% in CAIT's shares.
- Lakshmi aunty is a composite example (EXAMPLE tag on screen).
- Teaser for 6/6 makes no number claims.
