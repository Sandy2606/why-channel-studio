## ENGLISH · YouTube Short

**Title:** ₹7 Lakh Crore This Diwali: Where Does It All Go? #shorts

**Description:**
India's festive trade is expected to cross ₹7 lakh crore this season, Navratri to Diwali. Where does it all go? 🪔🛒

We followed ₹100 of goods sales, using the traders' own category estimates (CAIT):
🛒 Groceries & FMCG: ₹12. The biggest slice. Not gold.
💍 Gold, silver & jewellery: ₹10 · 📺 Electronics: ₹8
🧺 Appliances, ready-made clothes, gifts: ₹7 each
🏠 Home décor, furniture, sweets: ₹5 each
📦 Everything else: ₹34
🚚 Services (deliveries, cabs, catering) are part of the ₹7 lakh crore too: CAIT expects roughly ₹80,000 crore this year.
🏪 Last year, CAIT estimated 85% of festive trade came through mainline retail, especially traditional markets and non-corporate shops, and about 50 lakh temporary jobs.

These are traders' estimates, not official figures. But small shops are a huge part of the story.
