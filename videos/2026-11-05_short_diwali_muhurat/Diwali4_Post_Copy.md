## ENGLISH · YouTube Short

**Title:** Why India's Stock Market Opens on Diwali (for Just 70 Minutes) #shorts

**Description:**
This Sunday is Diwali, a holiday. But India's stock market will open for just 70 minutes. Why? 🪔📈

🕰️ It's called Muhurat trading. Muhurat means an auspicious time.
📒 For many trading families, Diwali marks the new accounting year, Samvat 2083. Old account books are closed and new ones are worshipped (Chopda Pujan), and traders often make a symbolic first investment at an auspicious time.
🏛️ The Bombay Stock Exchange began Muhurat trading in 1957. NSE, which started trading shares in 1994, holds it too.
⏰ Sun, 8 Nov 2026: pre-open 2:05–2:20 PM, trading 2:20–3:30 PM (main cash-equity window; other segments differ). Last year's session was one hour.
📊 In the last 11 Muhurat sessions (2015–2025), the Nifty 50 closed higher 9 times. But every move was under 1% either way (change vs the previous close). Festive mood, lower volumes. It's a ritual, not a forecast.
✅ The trades are real and follow normal settlement.

Not investment advice.
