## Fact checks (checked Nov 3, 2026)
- Sun 8 Nov 2026 Muhurat session (NSE/BSE via Zerodha bulletin, 27 Oct 2026): equity pre-open 2:05–2:20 PM; normal market 2:20–3:30 PM for non-CAS stocks (= 70 min); CAS stocks 2:20–3:15 + closing auction 3:20–3:35; F&O 2:20–3:40. "Trading from 2:20 to 3:30" is the headline equity window.
- 2025 session: 1:45–2:45 PM (60 min), pre-open 1:30 (PTI via Morung Express). Some outlets call 2026 the "first afternoon session"; that's wrong (2025 was also afternoon), so the script doesn't say it.
- Muhurat marks the start of the new Hindu accounting year, Samvat (Outlook Money). Samvat 2082 began with Diwali 2025, so Diwali 2026 starts Samvat 2083.
- Chopda Pujan = worship of account books (Angel One).
- BSE started the tradition in 1957 (Angel One, PTI). NSE was incorporated in 1992 but its equities (capital market) segment began trading on 3 Nov 1994 (Business Standard, "NSE's capital market segment turns 21"), so the many articles saying "NSE joined in 1992" are wrong. The script doesn't claim the year NSE held its first Muhurat session.
- Nifty 50 on Muhurat sessions 2015–2025: up 9 of 11 (down 2016 −0.14%, 2017 −0.63%); largest move +0.87% (2022). All moves under 1% (Angel One table).
- Trades settle normally: BTST proceeds from Nov 8 settle Nov 9 (Zerodha).
- Teaser (v2 wording: "festive trade expected to cross", a forecast, not measured spending): CAIT expects festive-season (Navratri → Diwali) sales to cross ₹7 lakh crore in 2026; last year ≈ ₹6.05 lakh crore (Business Today, 14 Oct 2026).
