# DELHI RAIN 1/6 · scene sheet v2 · "Evidence first, host as punctuation" · Nov 14 · APPROVED Nov 14 and BUILT (see QC_report_v2.md for what changed during the build: G5 replaced by a self-drawing schematic; the five adjustments; full-frame composition)

## Why v2
The user's review of v1 (Nov 14): the host fills half the Short with long stretches where nothing changes, so viewers swipe. v1 measured this way:
- **Host on screen:** ≈57 s of 116 s (49%).
- **Longest unchanged shot:** 28 s (the studio board).
- **Average shot:** ≈9 s.

GS 5/6, which worked, had one exhibit at a time, a reveal on each spoken word, documents and maps, and the second voice only as short punctuation.

## The new grammar
1. **Something new every ≈2 s:** a cut, a reveal, a camera move or a label landing on the spoken word. The test is the longest stretch with no visual change, which must be ≤ 2.5 s outside marked holds (measured with freezedetect at 2.0 s in QC).
2. **The host is punctuation, not the frame.** He appears in 5 short moments, ≈12 s in total (≈10%): the hook, the "Not proof" head shake, the "But here's what really changed" lean-in, the final question, and the loop. Each moment uses a different framing (medium, close, over-the-shoulder), so he reads as a person reacting, not a figure standing.
3. **Evidence is visible:**
   - The 1961 paper, the IITM report and the 2025 news reports appear as designed document and headline cards in our own typography, with source lines. No real mastheads or logos, and no reproduced pages (the C17 paper is CC BY-NC).
   - Dates flip, calendars count and the map flies.
4. **Same audio:** the approved voice, music and timings stay. **0 new credits.** Monday, November 16 is unchanged.
