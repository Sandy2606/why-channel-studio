## ENGLISH · YouTube Short

**Title**
Delhi Tried to Make It Rain in 1957. Here's What Happened 🌧️ #shorts

**Description**
Delhi sent a plane into the clouds in 2025 to make it rain. But it wasn't the city's first try. Not even close. 🤔

🧂 July 1957: scientists at Delhi's National Physical Laboratory mixed salt into water and sprayed it into the midday air from the ground, hoping rising air would carry it up to the clouds.
📊 Three monsoons, 18 trial runs: 12 leaned towards more rain, 2 went the other way, 4 couldn't tell. The scientists' own verdict: not enough to be sure. Keep testing.
❄️ Winter 1971–72: silver iodide this time. 11 days seeded, 11 left alone to compare. An IITM report says the seeded days showed a tendency towards more rain. A tendency. Not proof.
🌫️ What changed: those early trials were about more rain. In 2025, Delhi wanted rain for clean air. On Oct 28, 2025, no rain was recorded in the city (Noida 0.1 mm, Greater Noida 0.2 mm).

Nearly seventy years of trying. So why is it still so hard to make Delhi rain? Next: how something that looks like smoke is supposed to make a cloud rain.
