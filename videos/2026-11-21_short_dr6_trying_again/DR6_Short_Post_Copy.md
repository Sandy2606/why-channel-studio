## ENGLISH · YouTube Short

**Title**
Rain After Cloud Seeding in Delhi? What the Evidence Really Shows 🌧️ #shorts

**Description**
On November 14, a plane seeded the sky over Delhi, and light rain was reported. So did the seeding make it rain? 🤔

📄 The contractor's post-flight report links that flight to light rain and cleaner air. That's the operator's own report, not an independent scientific result.
🌥️ At the same time, a western disturbance was passing through, and air improved even in places the plane never flew over. Delhi's average AQI went 373 → 367 → 290 → 221 from Nov 13 to 16, which reports put down to stronger winds and the western disturbance. This evidence can't show the seeding caused it.
📅 More flights are planned for November 23–25.
💰 Two separate estimates from IIT Kanpur's director: about ₹60 lakh for the two October 2025 flights, and ₹25–30 crore for a hypothetical whole-winter programme.
🔬 IIT Delhi's preliminary, not peer-reviewed analysis: cloud seeding "cannot be recommended as a primary or strategic measure".

✅ The answer: in Delhi's winter, suitable clouds are often missing, and any relief rain brings to the air is only temporary. The lasting fix is cutting what goes into the air.

So if Delhi's clean air has to come from the ground… what's really filling its sky every winter?
