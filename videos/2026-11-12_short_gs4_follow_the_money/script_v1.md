(reuse 4.6)

8. [EXPLAIN] The Mughal empire was one of the **largest states** on Earth. ⏸ Under **Akbar**, his minister **Todar Mal** measured the land and set revenue by **survey**. (reuse 4.4)
9. [LAND] But kings come and go. **The system lasted.** (reuse 4.5, 1st part)
10. CHELLI [worried] And when things went **wrong**? (NEW) → chip 3/3
