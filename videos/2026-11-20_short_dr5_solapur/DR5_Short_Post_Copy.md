## ENGLISH · YouTube Short

**Title**
Solapur Did It. Why Can't Delhi? The Cloud-Seeding Experiment That Worked 🌧️ #shorts

**Description**
Scientists got more rain to fall near Solapur. So why can't Delhi copy them? 🤔

🌾 Solapur sits in a rain shadow; when you farm there, every bit of rain counts.
✈️ In 2017–19, IITM's CAIPEEX team released salt at the base of growing monsoon clouds, and picked which clouds to seed at random, like a coin toss.
📊 Result: about 18% more rain (a relative estimate from radar analysis; unseeded = 100, seeded ≈ 118), over 100 km² downwind, in the two hours after seeding, in suitable monsoon clouds. Peer-reviewed and published.
🗺️ Undivided Andhra Pradesh (today's AP + Telangana) ran its own programme in 2003–08: 12 districts, about 2,200 flights. But running a programme isn't the same as proving it worked; that's why Solapur's coin-toss design mattered.
☁️ Solapur had tall, wet monsoon clouds that were already growing. Delhi's November sky has none of that: seeding can push a cloud, it can't bring one.
🏔️ The WMO says the clearest evidence is for seeding cold mountain clouds in winter. Results depend on the kind of cloud, so what worked in Solapur can't simply be copied to Delhi.

So is Delhi trying again this winter? Next.
