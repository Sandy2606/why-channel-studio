## ENGLISH: audiovisual script
♻ = approved film voice line (tag `delhirain_film`). NEW = to be voiced.

**1** · NEW · HOOK
Scientists got more rain to fall near Solapur… ⏸ so why can't Delhi copy them?
- SCREEN: frame 1 is a split screen. Top: a monsoon cloud raining over farmland, pinned SOLAPUR. Bottom: Delhi's grey balcony sky, pinned DELHI. A "?" sits between them.

**2** · ♻ 5.2 · Solapur sits in a rain shadow. … Monsoon clouds pass over… and often don't give much. … When you farm here, every bit of rain counts.
- SCREEN: a drawn cross-section of the Western Ghats. Monsoon wind rises and rains on the coast side; the far side (Solapur) stays dry: RAIN SHADOW. A small India map pins Solapur.

**3** · ♻ 5.3 · From 2017 to 2019, IITM scientists flew into growing monsoon clouds here… and released salt at the base.
- SCREEN: the plane (G6 style) flies under a tall growing cloud; salt particles (drawn) rise into its base. Label: CAIPEEX · 2017–19 · SALT AT THE CLOUD BASE.

**4** · ♻ 5.4 · And they did it like a proper experiment. … Some clouds were seeded. Others were left alone. … Which was which was picked at random, like a coin toss, so the comparison was fair.
- SCREEN: a row of clouds; a coin flips for each and marks it SEEDED or LEFT ALONE. Counter: RANDOMISED · 150 SEEDED / 122 NOT.

**5** · ♻ 5.5 · The result: about 18 percent more rain, over 100 square kilometres downwind, in the two hours after seeding.
- SCREEN: two rain gauges, NOT SEEDED and SEEDED, with ≈18% MORE on the seeded one. Map patch: 100 KM² DOWNWIND · 2 HOURS. Label: RELATIVE · IN SUITABLE MONSOON CLOUDS.

**6** · ♻ 5.6 · That's real. … Peer-reviewed, and published.
- SCREEN: SOURCE card for Prabhakaran et al., *BAMS* 2023, stamped PEER-REVIEWED.

**7** · NEW · ★ [C16]
Undivided Andhra Pradesh ran its own seeding programme too, from 2003 to 2008… ⏸ twelve districts, about two thousand two hundred flights.
- SCREEN: the India map moves to undivided Andhra Pradesh; 12 district dots light up; a sortie counter runs to ≈2,200. Labels: 2003–08 · A PROGRAMME, NOT PROOF OF RESULTS. Source: *J. Weather Modification*, 2009.
- TE: "ఉమ్మడి ఆంధ్రప్రదేశ్" (see the Telugu script).

**8** · NEW
But running a programme isn't the same as proving it worked. ⏸ That's why Solapur's coin-toss design mattered.
- SCREEN: two columns. PROGRAMME: flights flown. RANDOMISED TEST: seeded vs not, compared fairly. The coin from line 4 lands on the second column.

**9** · ♻ 5.7 · So why can't Delhi just copy Solapur?
- SCREEN: **the one host turn**, at the board (note COPY SOLAPUR?).

**10** · ♻ 5.8 · Look at what Solapur had. … Tall, wet monsoon clouds… already growing. … Seeding gave them a push.
- SCREEN: the monsoon cloud painting (S1b, native pixels) with an upward push arrow. Labels: TALL · WET · ALREADY GROWING → SEEDING: A PUSH.

**11** · NEW
Now look up at Delhi's sky in November.
- SCREEN: cut to the grey balcony sky (P2 with smog).

**12** · ♻ 5.9 (from "None of that." to the end) · None of that. … Seeding can push a cloud. … It can't bring one.
- SCREEN: an empty dashed cloud outline over the smog; the push arrow from line 10 has nothing to push. Labels: CAN PUSH A CLOUD · CAN'T BRING ONE.

**13** · ♻ 5.10 · Even the WMO says the strongest evidence is for one thing: seeding cold mountain clouds in winter, for more snow. … Bigger claims, it says, "should be treated with suspicion."
- SCREEN: WMO SOURCE card (updated Jul 2026); snowy mountain clouds marked STRONGEST EVIDENCE; quote card "should be treated with suspicion". Small line: salt seeding: weaker evidence, depends on the clouds (WMO).

**14** · NEW · TEASE
So is Delhi trying again this winter? … Next.
- SCREEN: WHY? question card ("Is Delhi trying again?"), then the series slide: 1/6–4/6 WATCHED · **5/6 YOU'RE HERE** · 6/6 NEXT "Delhi is trying again. Has anything changed?", ending CTA.

**15** · NEW · LOOP
It all started with one experiment…
- SCREEN: back to frame 1.
