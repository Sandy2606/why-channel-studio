9. 09-india-diwali-rainbow-composite.jpg — 600 x 577, 94 KB
   Source image: https://earthsky.org/upl/2012/11/India_Diwali_fake-e1353258478199.jpeg
   (from the EarthSky article in item 3)
   The viral rainbow image: colour composite based on DMSP
   Operational Linescan System data, created in 2003 by NOAA
   scientist Chris Elvidge to highlight population growth over
   time — white = city lights visible before 1992; blue/green/red
   = lights that became visible in 1992, 1998 and 2003
   respectively. It is NOT a photograph of India on Diwali night.
   Low resolution as published (600 x 577); not upscaled.
