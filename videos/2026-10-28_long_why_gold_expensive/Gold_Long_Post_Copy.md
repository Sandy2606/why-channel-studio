## ENGLISH · YouTube

**Title:** Why Is Gold So Expensive? (It Came From Space)

**Description:**
In January 2026, one ounce of gold crossed $5,000 for the first time in history. It's just a metal: we can't eat it, we can't run our cars on it. So why does the whole world keep paying more and more for it? The answer starts somewhere you'd never expect. 🌌

⭐ Gold isn't made on Earth, or even in our Sun. It's made when dead stars (neutron stars) crash. Scientists watched one of those crashes in 2017, 130 million light-years away.
⛏️ All the gold ever mined is only about 222,000 tonnes: a cube roughly 22 metres on each side.
✨ Gold never rusts, so almost all of it is still around today, just changing hands.
🧪 Why gold became money: a chemist's periodic-table elimination leaves silver, platinum and gold, and gold wins.
🪨 A typical mine crushes a whole tonne of rock for about 1.5 g of gold, smaller than a peppercorn. The world mines only ~3,600 tonnes a year.
🏦 Countries are buying: central banks bought more than 1,000 tonnes a year from 2022 to 2024, versus ~470 before.
📈 The price went from ~$1,400 (2019) to over $5,000 (Jan 2026). It's around $4,000 now, still almost 3× 2019.
🇮🇳 And in India, gold is family: weddings, Akshaya Tritiya, Dhanteras (Nov 6).
