## ENGLISH
1. [conversational] Is your Diwali bonus a gift? ⏸ For some people, it's the **law**. For others, it really is just a gift. ⏸ And it all comes down to one number: **21,000**.
   → split screen "GIFT" | "LAW"; ₹21,000 slams in between
2. But first, a correction. ⏸ In our full video, I said this comes under the **Payment of Bonus Act, 1965**. I missed this, sorry. Since **November 2025**, it's part of a new law, the **Code on Wages**.
   → full-video thumbnail + "CORRECTION" stamp; 1965 clipping → Code on Wages clipping
3. India merged **four** old wage laws into this one. ⏸ Most bonus rules stayed the same. A few changed. I'll show you both.
   → four law cards merge into one; scoreboard header "OLD LAW · 1965 | NEW LAW · 2025"
4. Okay. The **21,000** test. ⏸ Meet Ravi. He packs boxes at a warehouse with **50** people, and earns **18,000** a month.
   → "RAVI · ₹18,000/month" card
5. That's **21,000 or less**. ⏸ So if he worked at least **30 days** this year, his company **must** pay him a bonus. Old law, new law… same answer.
   → OLD ✓ | NEW ✓ · "LAW"
6. Now meet Arjun. Software engineer, **45,000** a month. ⏸ That's **more** than 21,000, so this law doesn't cover him at all. ⏸ His Diwali bonus? That one really is a **gift**.
   → "ARJUN · ₹45,000/month" · OLD ✕ | NEW ✕ · "GIFT (company's choice)"
7. Surprised? ⏸ That's why so many office and IT workers have never heard of this rule. It was built to protect **lower-paid** workers.
8. So how much does Ravi get? ⏸ Here's the part most people miss. It's **not** counted on his full salary.
   → ₹18,000 with a "?" over it
9. Every state fixes a **minimum wage**, the lowest pay the law allows for a job. ⏸ It depends on how **skilled** the work is, and **where** you work. A city job gets a higher minimum than the same job in a village.
   → skill ladder (unskilled → highly skilled) × city / town / village
10. States must review it at least every **five years**, and top it up twice a year as prices rise. ⏸ And earlier, only some jobs had a minimum wage. Now, **every** job does.
    → OLD: some jobs | NEW: every job
11. Say the minimum wage for Ravi's job is **12,000**. ⏸ His bonus is counted on that **12,000**… not his 18,000.
    → ₹18,000 crossed → ₹12,000 (small note: "counted on your salary or the minimum wage, whichever is lower, but never below ₹7,000")
12. 8.33 percent, for twelve months… about **12,000** rupees. ⏸ Basically one extra month's pay. That's the minimum. In a good year, it can go up to 20 percent, about **28,800**.
    → ₹12,000 → up to ₹28,800
    → mid-video subscribe card (small, quiet)
13. So what actually **changed** in the new law? ⏸ Four things.
14. One: the bonus must go into your **bank account**. The old law said **cash**.
    → OLD: cash | NEW: bank credit
15. Two: small factories. The old law covered every factory, even one with **12** workers. ⏸ Now it's only places with **20 or more** people. So Suresh, in a tiny 12-person factory… just **lost** his legal bonus.
    → "SURESH · 12-worker factory" · OLD ✓ | NEW ✕
16. Three: that 21,000 line used to be written into the law itself. ⏸ Now the **government** sets it, and in **August 2026**, it kept it at **21,000**.
    → "₹21,000 · IN THE ACT" → "₹21,000 · SET BY GOVT, AUG 2026" (clipping)
17. Four: along with fraud, violence and theft, a conviction for **sexual harassment** can now cost you your bonus.
    → list, new line highlighted
18. The company has **eight months** after the year ends to pay. ⏸ For most companies, that's **November 30th**. Didn't get it? You can file a claim with your state's **labour department**.
    → calendar NOV 30 circled → "FILE A CLAIM · LABOUR DEPT."

## Fact checks
- Eligibility: wages ₹21,000/month or less (basic + DA), at least 30 days in the year, establishment with 20+ people (new Code s.41(2)); same ₹21,000 and 30 days under the old Act. Above ₹21,000: no statutory bonus (old and new).
- Calculation base = the lower of actual wage and the cap; cap = higher of ₹7,000 or the minimum wage (old Act s.12; new s.26(2) + notification of 25 Aug 2026). Ravi: 12,000 × 12 × 8.33% = 11,995 ≈ 12,000; × 20% = 28,800.
- Minimum wage: set by states/UTs by skill level and area/zone; reviewed at intervals not exceeding 5 years; VDA revised 1 Apr / 1 Oct; now covers all workers (earlier ~40%). ₹12,000/month is realistic in low-wage states (Odisha ₹462/day).
- Changes: cash → bank credit (s.39); every factory → 20+ only (s.41(2)); 21,000 in the Act → by notification (kept at 21,000, S.O. 4711(E), 25 Aug 2026, effective 21 Nov 2025); sexual-harassment conviction added (s.29).
- Deadline: 8 months after year end (s.39); 30 Nov for a March year-end. Claims: s.45 claims before the authority appointed by the appropriate government (state labour department for most private employers).
