ARTICLES (articles/)
1. 01-taxmann-bonus.pdf — 14 pages
   Taxmann, "[Analysis] Bonus Under the Code on Wages 2019 –
   Applicability | Allocable Surplus | Set-on and Set-off"
   Published 1 Sep 2026, updated 25 Sep 2026.
   Source: https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/bonus-under-code-on-wages-2019-allocable-surplus-set-on-set-off/
   Old-vs-new content, verified in the PDF text:
   - Ch. IV (ss. 26-41) replaces the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965
     from 21 Nov 2025; applicability now 20+ persons (s. 41(2)),
     vs every factory + 20+ establishments under the 1965 Act.
   - 1965 Act: Rs 21,000 sat in the "employee" definition
     (s. 2(13)), so above it there was no entitlement at all.
     Code: "employee" (s. 2(k)) has no wage ceiling; s. 26(1)
     requires 30 days' work, and s. 26(2) computes bonus for a
     person above the notified amount as if his wage were that
     amount or the minimum wage, whichever is higher —
     Taxmann's commentary reading: "Employee will not be
     deprived of bonus" (p. 4).
   - Unchanged: minimum 8.33% of wages earned or Rs 100,
     whichever is higher, even with no allocable surplus;
     maximum 20%; set-on/set-off to the 4th accounting year
     (s. 36). New: bank-account credit (s. 39(1), not cash);
     disqualification now also for conviction for sexual
     harassment (s. 29).
   WARNING — DATE LIMIT ON THIS PIECE: it states the law as on
   20 July 2026 and says (p. 4) the s. 26 ceiling "had not been
   prescribed" as at that date, and "Do not assume Rs. 21,000".
   That was true in July. It is superseded on the ceiling by
   item 3 below (25 Aug 2026 notifications). Do not use item 1
   alone for the Rs 21,000 figure.
   Renders: 01-taxmann-bonus-headline-p1-01.png (p. 1),
   01-taxmann-bonus-keyline-p4-04.png (p. 4, eligibility /
   wage-ceiling-as-cap section).

3. 03-scc-bonus-rules.pdf — 1 page
   SCC Online / SCC Times, "Centre notifies Bonus eligibility
   and Calculation rules under Code on Wages, 2019"
   Published 26 Aug 2026, updated 1 Sep 2026.
   Source: https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/08/26/bonus-rules-under-code-on-wages-eligibility-calculation/
   (URL slug: bonus-rules-under-code-on-wages-eligibility-
   calculation — this is the Aug 26 item you named.)
   Verified in the PDF text (whole article is p. 1):
   - "On 25 August 2026, the Ministry of Labour and Employment
     issued two notifications under the Code on Wages, 2019"
   - "employees earning up to Rs 21,000 per month will be
     eligible for bonus" — s. 26(1). The article body itself
     does NOT print the S.O. numbers; S.O. 4711(E) (eligibility)
     and S.O. 4710(E) (calculation) are the Gazette
     notification numbers for the two 25 Aug 2026 notifications
     it reports, shown in my banner note on p. 1, not in
     SCC's own text.
   - Bonus for wages over Rs 7,000 "will be calculated on
     Rs 7,000 per month or the Central Government's minimum
     wage, whichever is higher" — s. 26(2) (S.O. 4710(E)).
   - "Both notifications are deemed to have come into force
     retrospectively from 21 November 2025."
   NOTE ON THE TENSION WITH ITEM 1: item 1 (July law) reads
   s. 26 as a computation cap, not an eligibility bar, while
   item 3 phrases Rs 21,000 as eligibility. Both are in the
   pack as published; do not flatten them into one line on
   screen without that distinction.

   article, headline + Rs 21,000 + Rs 7,000 on one page).
