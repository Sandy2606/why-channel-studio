## Healthcare angle (Sandeep's view; verify every claim with sources before scripting)
- India's healthcare is more efficient and cheaper than many countries'.
- The US has a higher quality of life (less pollution, lifestyle), yet hospital visits are far more common in India.
- US diet → diabetes and other lifestyle diseases; US care is very costly.
- India: care is subsidised by the government, by insurance, or by hospitals cross-subsidising (charging those who can pay more, so those who can't are covered).
- India's huge population keeps a dense network of hospitals viable; the US has fewer people over a much larger area.
