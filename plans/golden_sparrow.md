# Series plan: Golden Sparrow (Season 1, Nov 8–14 2026)

Precedence level 3: applies to this series' videos; overrides a channel rule only where it was approved later or as an exception. Channel rules are cited by ID and not repeated.

- **[GS-01]** (P, user Nov 6, after rejecting a slideshow test) **Visual system:** animate information, not pictures. Three visual languages:
  - cinematic reconstruction: 2.5D depth parallax (never on people, → MO-02), slow dolly, atmosphere;
  - animated maps: Equal Earth world and regional maps, India's official outline (→ ART-05), colour fields and route lines;
  - evidence and data: document cards with a highlight sweep, a 100-square grid, counters, a people grid, bars with a reference line, flows with moving coins.
  - Reveals sync to narration words (EN Whisper word times; TE pause and character position).
  - 16:9 and 9:16 are composed separately; the 9:16 safe area is x 70–900, y 220–1500, with labels at least 34 px.
  - Telugu graphics are fully localised (glossary in `videos/2026-11-08_long_golden_sparrow_s1/visual_production_plan.md`).
  - Restrained treatment for famine, caste, child labour and battle.
- **[GS-02]** (P, approved GS 4/6, Nov 10) **Archival styling:** annotations as museum labels (ink plate, thin antique-gold rule, kicker and title, a leader line to the exact spot on the source, soft highlighter); date reveals as an ink cartouche with an antique-gold double rule on a period map; no rubber stamps, split-flap boards or neon (→ MO-03).
