# Idea bank: Indian history series (from Sandeep, Oct 31 2026), proposed as the next theme after Diwali week

- Memory lane: how India was before the Europeans came. Kingdoms and kings, wealth and trade, as recorded in books and sources.
- Arrival: when the first Europeans reached India and when the first British came. (Columbus never reached India: he sailed for India in 1492 and reached the Americas. Vasco da Gama reached Calicut in 1498. The English East India Company was chartered in 1600, and its first ship reached Surat in 1608.)
- What if the British never came: how would India look today? Present it as a sourced scenario, not a claim.
- Can India become a superpower again, as it was before the British era? Give a balanced view with data.
- It could run as multiple weekly series (pre-colonial kingdoms → arrival and conquest → what-if and the future).
- Pairs with the India vs US comparison idea (plans/ideas_india_vs_us.md).
